READ THE BOUNDARIES

What the rover knows.
What the simulator checks.

Onboard-style planning

Perfect range beams update a private grid. The planner sees that grid, its pose and its goal. Unknown cells are provisionally open; newly observed obstacles can force a new route.

Bounded motion model

Left and right side speeds determine linear and angular motion. Side speed, yaw and discrete speed changes are limited. Curved motion is integrated analytically.

A central safety shield

The simulator checks the entire swept body against ground-truth obstacles. Rejected motion ends the run at its last accepted state. This does not prove a physical stopping distance.

04 / BENCH INTERFACE

A frame, not a motor command.

This builder only produces protocol text. The separate firmware boots neutral, requires an explicit arm, and has sequence/CRC checks and a lease watchdog. No serial device is opened here.

Signed PWM units are not measured wheel speeds. A separate physical emergency stop, current sizing and human bench tests remain required.
